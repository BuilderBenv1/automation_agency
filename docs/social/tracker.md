# Performance Tracker + Review Rubric

The point of tracking isn't likes. It's finding the **pillar + hook + platform combinations that produce Quick-Audit submissions and calls**, then doing more of those and cutting the rest. You paste the numbers; the monthly read tells you what to change.

Keep it in one Google Sheet. Two tabs: **Posts** and **Monthly review**.

---

## Tab 1: Posts (one row per post)

| Column | What goes in it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Date | Post date | 2026-07-20 |

| Format | Text / Text+screenshot / Carousel | Text |
| Hook style | Contrarian / Number / Question / Story / How-to | Contrarian |
| CTA level | Soft / Med / Hard | Soft |
| Source tag | li / fb / fbg (blank if no link) | li |
| Reach / impressions | From the platform | 1,240 |
| Reactions | Likes etc. | 38 |
| Comments | Count | 11 |
| Saves | (LinkedIn/where shown) | 6 |
| Link clicks | To the Quick-Audit link | 9 |
| **Quick-Audits** | Submissions you can attribute (from the tagged inbox email) | 2 |
| **Calls booked** | Downstream of this post | 1 |
| Notes | Anything worth remembering | "reply thread went well" |

**The two columns that matter most are the bold ones.** Everything left of them is leading indicators; those two are the money.

### Where the attribution comes from
Quick-Audit CTAs use tagged links (`/quick-audit?src=li`, `?src=fb`, `?src=fbg`). Every submission emails you a notification whose subject is tagged, e.g. `Quick-Audit [LinkedIn]: Jane — Acme Ltd`, with a **Source** row at the top. Direct/untagged leads show as **Direct / unknown** — still log them, that's signal too.

## The review rubric

### Weekly — 15 minutes
- Paste the week's numbers into **Posts**.
- Note the tagged Quick-Audit emails against the post that likely drove them.
- Don't analyse yet. Just capture, while it's fresh.

### Monthly — 30 minutes
Answer four questions, write one sentence each:

1. **Which pillar drove the most Quick-Audits?** (Not reach — Quick-Audits.) → do more of it next month.
2. **Which hook style got clicks *and* leads?** Contrarian openers usually beat clever ones. → reuse the winning style.
3. **What got reach but no leads?** That's entertainment, not marketing. → keep only if it's cheap to make; otherwise cut.
4. **What flat-out didn't land?** → cut it from next month's calendar without sentiment.

Then rebuild next month's `calendar-30day.md` from `idea-bank.md`, weighted toward what won.

### What "good" looks like (rough, first 90 days)
- You're posting 3×/week without it hurting.
- At least one attributed Quick-Audit a month from social, trending up.
- One pillar clearly pulling ahead — that's your signal to lean in, not diversify.

Don't chase virality. A steady drip of the right people asking "can you look at my worst manual job?" is the entire win.

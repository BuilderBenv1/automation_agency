**Hero idea:** You probably don't need AI (Pillar: Honest take / myth-bust)
**Why this hero first:** it leads with your moat — the honest engineer who tells people when *not* to automate. It builds trust before any ask, and it's pure text, so nothing can stop you shipping it.

Paste each post as-is or tweak the voice. Suggested screenshots are optional — a text-only LinkedIn post is completely fine.

## Monday — LinkedIn (text-only, Soft CTA)

> Half the "AI automation" pitches I see could be a spreadsheet formula.
>
> Someone will quote you thousands to "add AI" to a job that's really just copying data from one app into another. That's not an AI problem. It's a two-apps-that-don't-talk problem, and it usually costs a few hundred quid to fix properly — once.
>
> Here's the honest test I use before building anything:
>
> Does the job need judgement — reading messy text, deciding, drafting a reply? Then AI might genuinely earn its place.
>
> Is it the same steps every time, just tedious? Then you don't need AI. You need plumbing. Zapier, Make, or a bit of n8n, and it runs forever without a monthly "intelligence" tax.
>
> The most expensive automation is the one that adds cleverness to a process you should have deleted.
>
> What's a job in your week that *feels* like it needs AI? Reply and I'll tell you honestly whether it does.

*Format note: no image needed. If you want one, a screenshot of a dead-simple 3-node n8n/Zapier flow works.*

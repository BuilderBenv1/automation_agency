# 30-Day Content Calendar — The Automation Agency

Four weeks. One hero idea per week, reshaped into a LinkedIn post, a Facebook page post, and a reactive Facebook group action. Instagram cut.

**How to read a row:** each hero produces three surface posts. LinkedIn and Facebook page are scheduled; the group action is *reactive* — do it when a matching real thread appears, otherwise let it slide to next week.

**CTA levels:** Soft (engage/save) · Med (Quick-Audit, `?src=…`) · Hard (book/pricing).
**Source tags:** LinkedIn `?src=li` · FB page `?src=fb` · FB group `?src=fbg`.

## Monthly rhythm baked in

- **Week 1** opens with your strongest pillar (honest take) — it earns trust before you ask for anything.
- **One medium CTA per week**, alternating LinkedIn ↔ Facebook page, so the Quick-Audit drumbeat is steady but never every post.
- **Build-in-public / Community** slot in as a 4th post on any week you have the energy, or replace a hero if a client win or a broken build is more interesting that week.
- Roll the **community spark** ("what task would you pay to never do again?") in whenever engagement dips — it reliably wakes an audience up.

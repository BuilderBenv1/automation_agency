# Evergreen Idea Bank

So you never face a blank week. Pull one, make it the hero, reshape it three ways. Grouped by pillar. Add to this list every time a client conversation sparks something — the best ideas come from real jobs, not brainstorms.

---

## Honest take / myth-bust (your moat — over-use this pillar)

- Half the "AI automation" pitches could be a spreadsheet formula.
- The most expensive automation fixes a process you should have deleted.
- You don't have an AI problem — you have a copy-paste-between-two-apps problem.
- "Can you build me an AI agent?" Usually no. Here's what you actually need.
- No-code tools are brilliant — until they aren't. How to know when you've outgrown Zapier.
- "Automate everything" is bad advice. Automate the boring, repeatable 20% first.
- Why I sometimes tell people *not* to hire me.
- AI won't fix a broken process. It'll just do the broken thing faster.
- The honest test for whether a job needs AI or just plumbing.
- "We tried automating and it broke" — usually means nobody planned for the edge case. Here's the fix.
- Subscriptions vs one-off builds: when a monthly "AI" fee is quietly robbing you.
- Chatbots that annoy customers vs ones that actually help — the one design difference.

## Teardown (your signature — concrete, screenshot the real thing)

- A dental practice answering the same 6 booking questions by phone, all day → the bot that took it over.
- Rekeying every website order into Xero by hand → gone.
- A customer enquiry becomes a CRM record + a Slack ping + a templated reply, untouched by a human.
- Chasing invoices manually → an automation that nudges late payers politely, on schedule.
- A weekly report built from 4 spreadsheets by hand → assembled automatically every Monday 7am.
- WhatsApp messages from customers → sorted, tagged and routed to the right person.
- A lead form that used to sit in an inbox → instantly qualified, logged and followed up.
- Manual appointment reminders → automatic across SMS/WhatsApp/email, no-shows down.
- Copying data between a CRM and an accounts package twice a day → one-directional sync, zero typing.
- Scraping the same public data by hand each week → scheduled, cleaned, dropped into a sheet.

## Proof (ration — slice each win several ways, get permission to name)

- Marmadbir: messaging cost down ~65% — and why it wasn't the AI.
- PlusRooms: an automation replaced a full day of manual work.
- Punthub: runs unattended, every day, nobody logs in.
- The "before" state of a client who now runs on autopilot (the pain, told honestly).

- "This has run for X months with zero intervention" — the real goal of good automation.

## Plain-English explainer (flex — use when no fresh teardown)

- What "n8n" actually is, no buzzwords.
- "AI agent" vs "chatbot" vs "automation" — what you're actually buying.
- What SPF/DKIM/DMARC mean, and why your emails land in spam without them.
- What an "API" is, explained with a real-world analogy.
- Zapier vs Make vs n8n — plain-English differences and when each fits.
- What a "webhook" is and why it makes things instant.
- Where AI genuinely helps in a small business — and where it's a gimmick.
- What a "process audit" is and why it saves money before you build anything.

## Build-in-public / personal (humanise the solo engineer)

- What I'm building this week and the one bit that's fighting me.
- Ten years in and I still start every build with "should this exist at all?"
- A thing that broke in production and what it taught me.
- Why I run my own website on the exact stack I build for clients.
- The unglamorous truth about automation: 80% is understanding the process, 20% is code.
- A tool I stopped using and what I switched to.
- How I decide what to charge — and why it's fixed-price, not hourly.

## Community spark (cheap reach — use to wake the audience up)

- "What's the one task you'd pay to never do again? I'll tell a few of you exactly how I'd automate it — free."
- Poll: what eats the most time — data entry / reporting / chasing people / scheduling?
- "Reply with your industry and I'll name one automation almost everyone in it is missing."
- "What's the most annoying app in your stack? Let's see if it plays nicely with others."
- "Show me your worst spreadsheet. I'll tell you what I'd automate first." (great with the `/tools/social` or `/quick-audit` tie-in)

## Quick reshaping reminders

- **LinkedIn:** hook line first, short paragraphs, one idea, light question/CTA at the end.
- **Facebook page:** warmer, shorter, local; savings and plain outcomes over jargon.
- **Facebook group:** never the hero verbatim — a helpful answer to a *specific* thread, no link.
- **Visual:** always a byproduct screenshot (workflow, result, before/after), never something made for social.
